What the boxes are

detector box passed to the tracker (measured)
detector box removed by the turf filter (measured)
detector box below conf 0.4 (measured, not used)
detector box, video without tracker (Wadi Degla windows)
tracker output T## (a measured detection + temporary ID)
engine PREDICTED position (nothing detected)
ground truth (SoccerTrack annotation)
T## are temporary ByteTrack IDs, not player identities. ByteTrack's internal predictions for lost tracks are not exported, so every T## box is a detection. Raw per-tile boxes (before the tile merge) were never stored.
